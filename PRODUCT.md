# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Teams estimating work together through a shared room link, without creating accounts.
They need private estimates, a simultaneous reveal and a clear view of the spread.

## Product Purpose

Hanko is room-based planning poker. It keeps votes hidden until reveal and lets a team
compare estimates, discuss the result and start another round with as little setup as
possible.

## Brand Personality

Clear, welcoming and playful.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards and repeated card grids.
- Decorative motion or playful elements that obscure the planning flow.
- Build labels that do not identify the code revision actually deployed.

## Design Principles

- Keep starting a room as the primary action; instructions should support rather than
  compete with it.
- Make vote privacy and reveal timing easy to understand.
- Use the hanko's ink, paper and stamp language without sacrificing legibility.
- Let cats add personality without blocking controls or estimates.
- Identify production builds with the exact source revision used to create them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep actions keyboard reachable and named, preserve visible focus, respect reduced-motion
preferences, support touch layouts, and maintain readable contrast in both locales.
